Mobile Testing Instructions

Option 1: Live Voice Input (Chrome/Safari)

  1. Tap the green Start Listening button.
  2. Grant microphone permissions when prompted by your browser.
  3. Speak commentary clearly into your phone mic (e.g. "Rohit hits a four off Shaheen").

Option 2: Instant Mobile Preset Buttons

If you are in a noisy environment or testing quickly:

  • Tap any of the scrollable Quick Presets under the voice panel (e.g. 🚀 6 Runs, ☝️ Wicket).
  • The app will automatically update the scorecard, play sound effects, and display haptic/visual feedback.

Browser Compatibility Tip

Web Speech Recognition works best on Chrome for Android and Safari on iOS (14.5+). If speech API is restricted on your mobile browser, use the touch preset buttons!
